Play as an app

On iPhone or iPad, open the game in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if offered. Launch from the new icon for a view without browser controls.

On Android, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen option. Use a secure game link; the local network preview may not offer installation. Keep your current browser tab if you need its saved progress.
