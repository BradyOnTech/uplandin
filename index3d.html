FIELDCRAFT

A little know-how

W A S D Walk · Shift Run · Q Whistle · M Survey map

Handle the dog: Z Whoa (stop it, or steady it on point) · X Hunt on (release it, or relocate from a point) · C Cast the way you face · V Dead bird where you look. Commands carry as far as your whistle.

F Toggle aim · Space Shoot · R Reload.
Mouse: hold right button to aim, left button to shoot.

On a trackpad, use F to raise the gun and Space to fire. If mouse lock is unavailable, drag to look and swing.

Drag left to walk; farther to run. Drag right to look.

Hold Shotgun to raise it, drag to swing, then release to shoot. At the screen edge, keep holding to continue the swing; move inward to stop. For another shot, press and swing again. Release over Lower to cancel, or tap Lower to put the gun down.

Whistle recalls your dog; whistle again at heel to send it hunting. Whoa stops the dog or steadies it on point. Dog ▸ opens Hunt on, This way (cast the way you face) and Dead bird (hunt where you look).

Turn your phone sideways for a wider view of the field.

You can fire whenever the gun is raised. Swing ahead of crossing birds; let distant birds go.

Your dog searches on its own. Whistle recalls it; whistle again once all dogs are at heel to send them hunting. A fallen bird is recovered and brought to hand automatically.

Work the edges into the wind. When your dog holds point, walk in steadily.

Play as an app

On iPhone or iPad, open the game in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if offered. Launch from the new icon for a view without browser controls.

On Android, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen option. Use a secure game link; the local network preview may not offer installation. Keep your current browser tab if you need its saved progress.
