Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: Production character art target for a browser low-poly upland hunting game. This is a concept sheet, not a screenshot or a finished runtime asset.
Primary request: Create one restrained, exceptionally well-designed low-poly adult German Shorthaired Pointer character sheet. The animal must look athletic, serious and natural, with believable canine anatomy rather than a cartoon or generic dog. This will guide a procedural Three.js skinned mesh.
Composition: landscape concept sheet with the SAME mature liver-and-white GSP shown in three views at consistent proportions: a large strict side view on point with one forepaw raised; a front three-quarter standing view; a smaller strict side neutral standing view. Four limbs per dog with correct hocks and wrists. Clean uncluttered warm-grey background and soft studio light; every dog fully visible with breathing room. Small simple title 'GSP — FORM TARGET' only.
Anatomy: chest reaching approximately to elbows, oval rib cage with real lateral volume, taut rising abdomen with moderate athletic tuck, defined but restrained withers and loin, short strong muscular hindquarters with stifle and hock, slender lower legs, compact oval paws. Muscular neck rises cleanly from withers and brisket, throat curves into jaw rather than forming a triangular membrane. Long sculpted head with shallow stop, natural broad nasal bridge, substantial but not heavy tapered muzzle, close lips, small dark eyes, low folded ears, restrained adult expression. Tail of moderate docked hunting length pointing straight back/slightly above horizontal. Point pose intent and balanced: head forward, weight supported naturally, lifted paw relaxed at wrist.
Style: Sophisticated naturalistic low-poly game art with deliberately placed broad planes and subtle smooth interpolation around muscles. Warm ivory body, deep rich liver head and several large asymmetrical liver patches, restrained fine ticking. No photoreal fur. Surface pattern must not disguise the form. About the visual complexity of a carefully authored 3000–5000 triangle game character, not a faceted origami shape. No giant eyes, no smile, no baby proportions, no separate cylindrical limbs, no inflated ham-shaped thighs, no giraffe neck, no blocky feet. Consistent game-ready proportions and silhouette in all views.
