# Quail Fields authored world

Quail Fields now has one physical property with a connected hunting route, dedicated prairie vegetation, an actual windmill, and detailed ground that follows the hunter anywhere on the property. This is an implementation milestone, not a declaration that the art meets the reference games yet.

**Shared geography**

The property remains 1,200 × 700 shared map yards (1,097 × 640 meters). The South Gate and West Track drops and all 28 bird-cover rectangles remain unchanged. Both access tracks bend through the same entry cover and still meet at the original junction. New shared trail segments connect that junction to the Old Windmill and return by the eastern field to the South Track. Sampled curves round the bends while retaining exact shared junctions and endpoints. The 2D map and 3D trail therefore describe the same loop; no bird behavior or career rules changed.

The landform is Quail-specific: broad prairie shoulders, a low northern rise, and one continuous shallow drainage. Smaller nearby folds create a reveal on each entry route without mountainous terrain. Its centerline is authored in property coordinates, and its bed is depressed by 2.7 meters. Terrain, surface moisture, grass, shrubs, trees and trails all sample the same stable property coordinate system. Selecting a different drop changes only the local render transform.

**Art direction and asset kit**

The environment is organized around sage and ochre grass masses, cooler trees in the drainage, dusty two-track ruts, and weathered farm materials. Wide views stay open enough to read a working dog. Tree stands frame the first cover from either drop and continue along the drainage and outer field shoulders.

The dedicated Quail environment replaces the generic grass, flora, and prop scatter only on this map. Its reusable kit contains narrow curved short-grass and nesting-cover blades, distant grass silhouettes, fine-leaved sand sage, branched sand-plum shrubs, irregular deciduous crowns and trunks, boundary fence posts and wire, an open gate, a farm pickup, and a lattice windmill with a slowly turning wheel and stock tank. Geometry remains editable in the source builders. The world assets do not require external textures or network requests.

The truck has a distinct hood, cab, glazed windows, open bed, tailgate, wheels/hubs, bumpers, lights, and field equipment. Gate leaves open away from the walking entrance. Windmill tower bracing, ladder, tail and rotor are separate recognizable forms. Static landmark geometry is merged by material so individual braces do not cost separate draw calls.

**Terrain and detail management**

The former 480-meter square detailed plate is replaced by 128-yard terrain tiles covering the entire property. Near tiles use 64 subdivisions (about 1.83 meters); distant tiles use 16. Shared samples match exactly, and downward skirts close differences between detail levels. Four continued heightfield bands surround the fence so either drop can look outward without seeing a plane edge or sky underneath the ground.

Grass and low cover are instanced in 40-yard cells and culled by distance and camera frustum. Trees are merged by stand into two meshes, one for bark and one for crowns. High and lite tiers use the same deterministic plant candidates and the same hunting cover. Lite removes some close blade instances and shortens their detailed range; it does not remove cover from the hunting simulation. Distant grass and shrub silhouettes preserve the structure of the field beyond close detail. Grass uses per-plant distance fades across chunk boundaries, a small wind bend with stable point phases, and a normal bias that represents light passing through thin leaves without black backfaces.

Ground color continues below the vegetation, separating cool living sward, warm nesting cover and exposed litter. Shared property-coordinate surface detail affects both terrain and track materials; fine grain fades in the distance to limit shimmer. The two-track ribbon follows the real heightfield along every shared trail, with paired worn ruts, a grass center and shoulders blending into the field. Its cross sections interpolate neighboring tangents so rounded bends do not split apart. An indexed local trail corridor keeps terrain and vegetation generation inexpensive as authored curve samples increase.

**Primary daylight**

Quail Fields overrides only its morning/noon light treatment: a warm 28-degree primary sun, cooler opposite fill, neutral sky bounce, and greener distant layers. Other maps retain their original time-of-day recipes. Cloud groups surround the actual property approaches; the old cloud bank had been composed entirely around the southern staged camera and left the normal northern entry sky empty. Smaller groups now leave breathing room around the treetops.

**Navigation interface**

`LandscapeModel.worldBounds()` provides the property boundary in local meters. `QuailEnvironmentSystem.collisionCircles()` provides tree trunks and entrance lane fences, and `LandmarksSystem.collisionCircles()` provides the pickup, gates, windmill tower and stock tank. These are small physical obstacle approximations; bushes and grass remain traversable cover. Quail hunter movement resolves contact in bounded nine-centimeter steps so a slow frame cannot skip through a thin lane fence.

**Tree and shrub silhouette pass**

The later bounded woody-vegetation pass replaces the repeated tree sculpture with three growth forms. Upright interiors, broad shoulders and outward-leaning edges give each existing stand a different profile. Each tree has four or five unequal crown masses and visible branching forks; its stable property seed varies the outline. Sand-plum shrubs now have three unequal low masses growing from a shared woody base. This reduces small repeated pads without adding trees or shrubs. The habitat, trails, drops, candidate positions and tree collision generation are unchanged.

**Grass and ground integration pass**

The subsequent grass pass replaces the broad repeated fans with narrower uneven blades, low arching leaves and three loose basal groups. A stable variation shader changes each blade's height, reach and angle without adding draw groups; its seed depends on the plant's rotation and scale, so changing truck drops does not rearrange the blades. Muted leaf bases brighten toward their tips. The short, tall and distant geometries retain their 27, 33 and 3 triangle budgets. Grass adds 3,024 bytes of shared vertex attributes, no textures and no materials. It does add per-vertex shading work and a separate shader variant from sage; this pass did not measure the resulting frame-rate or energy cost.

An [isolated grass audit](quail-world-renders/grass-ground-pass/grass-only-invariants.json), performed before the later windmill trail edits, compared the previous and revised environment. All instance matrices and instance colors matched exactly within each quality tier: 330,384 High instances and 221,888 Lite instances. All 83 trunk collisions also matched, and disposal cleared the scene. This establishes that the grass change itself preserves placement. The combined checkpoint includes a separate windmill bypass whose trail mask can change plants beside that route.

The ground pass removes the conspicuous directional ripples, reduces broad brightness variation and filters fine grain. The track shoulder now fades into the actual terrain with vertex alpha, while the ruts remain opaque. This retains one track material and draw group, but uses transparent blending without depth writes for that overlay. The former hard triangular color boundary is absent in the reviewed ground view.

The [grass and ground manifest](quail-world-renders/grass-ground-pass/manifest.json) records immutable checkpoint `boot3d-BF9dB22_.js`, source hashes and original capture metadata. **Current assessment and remaining checks**

The later [access and track checkpoint](quail-access-review/README.md) connects both inset entrances to closed boundary gates, extends their approach tracks, and blends the two branch junctions. Roads now match the actual near/far terrain triangles, including tile-boundary ownership. Build 4533 passes 375 tests, TypeScript and build; four exactly matched staged views and both 83-meter ordinary entrance walks passed in Chrome 148 using a documented diagnostic browser scheduling flag. Default-browser startup failures are retained, and the capture setting provides no new performance evidence. Mixed terrain detail boundaries can still produce a small step or pop.

The entry view is substantially more coherent than the original generic scatter: warm cover and cool ground form readable masses, trees frame an actual low rise, and the track provides a continuous route. The ground and track detail is visible in rendered gameplay. The windmill and outer field follow the same materials and landform rather than existing only in a favorable entry screenshot.

This remains a provisional art candidate. The woody pass makes broad silhouettes cleaner, exposes branch forks and makes shrubs read as continuous low cover. The grass and ground pass reduces repeated upright fans, calms the soil and removes the visible track shoulder seam while retaining readable warm cover. Close grass remains angular and sparse-looking, nearby soil is very smooth, and dark lower crown masses still limit the finish. The farther field composition is less authored than the two approaches. The pickup still straddles the road and would benefit from a derived roadside parking position. This does not yet justify a Firewatch or Pinetrail quality claim. Further work should refine plant groupings and field transitions while keeping the Southern Plains scale and open hunting sightlines.

The world evidence establishes arrival, working cover, point, a full normal-input walk around the windmill loop, and controlled inspections elsewhere on the property. Complete hunts are recorded in the separate gameplay validation. Further visual review of moving-camera detail transitions and final browser/device performance remain part of acceptance. Mobile hardware performance is not established by the lite setting or by desktop headless screenshots.
